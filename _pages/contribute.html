Contribute to the Blog

Want to share your thoughts, poetry, stories, or ideas? You can contribute to this blog!

How to Contribute

✉️ Submit via Form

Fill out the form below to submit your contribution:

💻 Submit via GitHub

If you're familiar with GitHub, you can contribute directly:

  1. Fork the repository at Lxsoftroxs.github.io
  2. Add your content to the appropriate folder (_poetry, _posts, etc.)
  3. Create a pull request with your contribution
  4. Wait for review and approval

Submission Guidelines

  • Keep content respectful and appropriate
  • Original work only (or properly attributed)
  • No spam or promotional content
  • All submissions are subject to review before publishing
