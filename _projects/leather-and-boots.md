## The goal

Build, rebuild and customize durable leather goods, especially footwear, instead of buying finished ones. This is the one project where my hands are actually on the material.

## Wolverine BLVD rebuild

So far I have:

- taken the sole and insole partly apart and pulled the foam insole
- cut and fitted a leather insole and started hand-stitching it in
- cut cork filler for the sole cavity
- reused the original wooden heel parts, and worked with rubber heel stock, nails and contact cement

I also researched sealing that won't darken the leather.

## Other boot work

- **Wolverine 1000 Mile speed-hook repair.** The plan is a veg-tan backing patch, skived and saddle-stitched, with the new hook set slightly off the stretched hole.
- **Vibram 430 full sole and heel (size 10).** The cheapest I found were about $14–15 for the sole and $13 for the heel.
- **Used Thursday boots.** Only the welted models are worth buying for resoling. The glued ones aren't.
