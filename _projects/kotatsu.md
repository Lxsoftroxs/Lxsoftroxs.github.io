## The goal

A proper kotatsu: a low table with a heater mounted under the top, a thick quilt draped over the frame, and a topper on the quilt. You put your legs under it and the quilt traps the heat. It heats you instead of the room, which suits a cold house.

## The pieces

| Piece | Status | Notes |

| Quilt | In transit | Reversible "Golden Corn" quilt from JaipurFabric |
| Heater | In transit from Japan | METRO MSU501H-K, 500 W, 100 V |
| Step-down converter | Not bought | Japanese 100 V heater on US 120 V needs a converter rated 1,000 W+ for headroom |
| Table + topper | Not bought | Real wood. The topper has to match or slightly exceed the frame (75–80 cm, about 30" standard) |

## Why ship a quilt from India

JaipurFabric charges US buyers roughly **3× the India price** (₹8,999 vs ₹2,999 for the same quilt). Buying at the India price and using a package forwarder gets around that. US duty should be about 14–15% now that the IEEPA tariffs have been struck down. The catch is that forwarders bill by box size, so a 2.5 kg quilt gets charged as 6 kg. Vacuum-compressing it is the main way to cut that.

## Table options

- **DIY:** one 3/4" × 2 × 4 ft maple plywood panel cut into two 24" squares (table and topper), 15" pine legs, dark stain. About **$51** (walnut panel about $75, no stain needed).
- **Ready-made:** a 30 × 30" dark walnut beech top for about $159.
- **Used:** local ReStores and thrift shops for furniture or lumber.

**Best-case total** for quilt, heater and DIY table is about **$170**, before the converter.
