Is that an Insryption Reference!?

Weeks spent thinking about myself, I had previously thought that I knew what I needed to know to become better. I kept a nuanced perspective to have a standable argument and world view that could shift or hold firm among different ideals. As I said before, one must stand by their fundamental conceptions otherwise every gust would cause the foundation to fold. My internal world told me I was too much to be around, but also that individualism was a western ideal that wouldn’t stand under pressure from non biased sources about the need for social living in humanity. Logic derives that either I should be single forever or that I will find someone who can tolerate me. Further pushing the logical results from the fundamentals I developed, I came to believe that someone who saw all of me and understood me would be all I needed to have a partner. Once put into practice the conclusions contradicted with the results leading to the proposition being incorrect. My whole world view crumbled into dust. In the absence of an empirically backed up hypothesis, I went to people who had seen more of life and collected their findings; the sceintifc community. An extensive amount of hours reading papers and researching relationship dynamics and statistical averages, I found solice in intellectualism. My theory of sociology couldn’t explain the concurrent phenomenon so I discarded it and now carry the amalgamation of those who came before. 

What did I discover? Well concepts like Wu Wei and the ideals of enactment versus intellectualizing. Basically to live in my authentic self while taking responsibility to hold the bad within, not to suppress it or put it on someone else, or even to change it, rather to just allow it to be within me and not outside of me. With the counter balance that my intellectualism isn’t a safety net or a shield or way of avoiding emotions, it is my emotions. This is the way I think and I need to embrace it and enjoy the way I process. The main thing that I learned was that I need to stop looking for the perfect person to be or the perfect person to accept me. Both western and eastern science show that understanding yourself to a greater degree helps with maintaining longer relationships, I spent a lot of time believing that but also believing I could accomplish self actualization and growth alongside someone. So I seek to find my authentic self, the person I’m comfortable being, because in doing so I find an internal happiness but also a version of myself who fits within my moral compass. My actions that cause harm I see cause harm and by finding a comfortable place in my head will be a space in which I act with my most thought and best intention to the point that mistakes still made will be inevitable but survivable. TLDR I discovered that your authentic self isnt you plus all the bad, its the person youre comfortable being. I dont know maybe thats more obvious to others but I spend a lot of my time preforming and acting under others expectations so I missed that in my development. 

My isolation is usually pretty harmful, well to put it another way its very self-harmful. As the title so greatly introduces the topic, the snake that eats itself; ouro boros. I spend so much time pouring over myself to find gaps and issues and ways to reformat and rebuild. However, its a cannibalisation, in the constant self reflection and reflection of self reflection I consume myself over and over again. Nothing new is gained, no new nutrition is introduced to the system so I can’t expect myself to grow. I then would feast on others, consuming just to build myself better, instead of more of a symbiotic relationship. I have been a leach many times, now there is a bit of an on the nose joke about autofellatio here but thats nasty. . . jk unless? But yeah I paint myself as the villain while still being the villian, its like damn wow nice you recognize what you are, why dont you do something about it? I’m working on it I swear, I haven’t stopped working on it, I just need to do it from a new perspective. I wanna be able to do more then just work on myself but its all I really have at the end of the day. 

Who am I comfortable being, while also avoiding the innate instinct to avoid things that are uncomfortable. Well I’d like to be seen and appreciated again and again as thats what reassurance is but I’m also uncomfortable depending so hard on others when they can’t support that. So I am comfortable sharing my art with those close to me and seeing how they respond to it. I am comfortable with criticism from those I respect, I am uncomfortable with my overwhelming internal feelings of wanting a relationship. I’m comfortable enjoying the things I like while sharing them, and I’m comfortable hearing new things from other people. So I am comfortable with the aspects of a relationship but not the way I handle it. I would be comfortable by maintaining my sense of self and my partners sense of self so that I could explore and they could as well, its like splitting up to see more of the world and then coming back together to revisit paths traveled alongside someone. Instead of getting dragged by your hand into uncomfortable territory. I am uncomfortable about my grades and my future, I am comfortable when I am putting in the work to learn it while not acknowledging the grades. So I want to leverage my love for learning to make myself feel more comfortable with my grades. I am comfortable writing but uncomfortable about recognizing parts of what I think about in writing, so I will hold those thoughts but I will not bring them to the page. I am uncomfortable in my room when I have nothing to do and uncomfortable outside when I have nothing to do. The aimlessness brings anxiety and uncomfortableness in pure existence. I am comfortable with dreams of owning a dog, I am comfortable with ideas of leaving this country to live among a government more aligned with my ideals. I am comfortable with these being motivators for current progress. I am uncomfortable being alone, but I am alone right now and I am comfortable. So I am uncomfortable being alone without any way to create or learn, or maybe I’m just uncomfortable with the concept of being alone. I feel uncomfortable not loving myself, I think its time that I started, it annoys me a lot that I haven’t gotten there yet. 
This wasn’t very poetic or like very preformative, which feels odd for me but I’m happy with what I wrote, cause I really do think I’m doing it for myself above all.
